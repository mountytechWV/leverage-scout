Searching

Type anything: a stock ticker (NVDA), an ETF ticker (SOXL), a company (Tesla), a sector or industry (semiconductors, biotech) or an issuer (Direxion). Several words narrow it down, for example nvda inverse or gold 3x.

Click an underlying ticker in the table to see every fund on it. Click an ETF ticker to open its chart (hover for the full fund name).

Filters (on the main page)

⚡ Day Trade
Only funds with average dollar volume of $25M+, price $5+ and an average daily range of 4%+ (20-day averages). Turns on biggest-volume-first sorting.
☆ Favorites
Star any fund, then use this to show only your starred funds.
Direction & Leverage
Long or Inverse, and pick one or more multiples (2x, 3x...).
Type, Sector, Industry, Issuer
Industry list follows the sector you pick.
Avg vol / ADR
Set a minimum share volume or daily range.
★ Save as my default
Leverage Scout opens with your current filters and sort every time.

What the columns mean

Avg Vol / Avg $ Vol
Average shares and dollars traded per day over the last 20 trading days.
Liquidity
High $100M+ a day, Good $20M+, Thin $2M+, Illiquid under $2M.
ADR %
Average daily range, high vs low, over 20 days. Higher means bigger intraday moves.
Exp % / AUM / Since
Expense ratio, assets under management and inception date, when available.

Click any column header to sort. Click it again to flip the order.

Exporting

Export CSV

Good to know

Leveraged and inverse funds reset daily, so over days and weeks their returns drift from the stated multiple. They are built for short holds.

Install as an app

Put Leverage Scout on your home screen or taskbar so it opens in its own window like a regular app. It's the same site, so it always has the latest fund list.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Tap Add. Leverage Scout now opens full screen from its icon.
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install. Samsung Internet: menu → Add page to → Home screen.
  1. Chrome: click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, then Install. No icon? Menu ⋮ → Cast, save and share → Install page as app.
  2. Edge: menu ··· → Apps → Install this site as an app. Safari (Mac): File → Add to Dock.
  3. To keep it handy, right-click its taskbar icon (Windows) or Dock icon (Mac) and choose Pin / Keep in Dock.
On iPhone and iPad the home-screen app keeps its own favorites, separate from Safari. Use Back up in Safari and Restore in the app to bring them over.
